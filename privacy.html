Privacy Policy

Last updated: REPLACE_DATE. Contact: REPLACE_EMAIL

NalandaKnowledgeBook stores your profile (name, class, school, language), subjects, study history and the API key you enter only on your own device. We do not run a server and we do not collect, sell or share your data.

When you generate a study plan or level check, the text you type or upload is sent directly from your device to the AI provider you chose (for example Google, OpenAI, Anthropic or Groq) using your own API key. That provider's privacy policy applies to that data. Do not enter personal or sensitive information in your notes.

If you choose Google sign-in, Google tells the app your name and email address. They are kept only on your device, and Google’s own privacy policy applies to the sign-in. If you pick a folder on a computer, your topics and chats are saved there as files you can read and delete.

AI answers can be wrong. Please check important facts with your teacher or textbook.

Children under 13 should use this app with a parent or guardian, who should create and manage the API key.

To delete your data, clear the app's storage in Android settings or uninstall the app.
